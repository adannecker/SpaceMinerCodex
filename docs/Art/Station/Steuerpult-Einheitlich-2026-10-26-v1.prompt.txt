Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: implementable 3D prop design concept sheet for the Unity space survival game SpaceMiner.
Primary request: Design ONE standardized command-room standing control console. The same physical console is used for drone management, power supply, station planning, research, and manufacturing. Only the screen contents and front function plaque change. This is a tangible realistic modular hard-surface industrial device that can be modeled faithfully, not an abstract UI painting.
Style/medium: exceptionally clean high-quality PBR 3D game-prop design render, realistic manufactured construction, restrained utilitarian near-future spacecraft engineering, crisp readable silhouettes and legible graphic design. Believable sufficient support structure; straightforward bevelled panels and reusable parts suitable for an optimized Unity model.
Composition: wide landscape professional concept board. Left 56%: large front-left three-quarter hero view of the full console including floor contacts, in a minimal dark space station interior with a hint of hexagonal wall panels. Hero device fills most of its area. Right 44%: clean smaller orthographic front and right-side views of exactly the SAME device, with sparse dimension lines. Bottom a narrow strip of five small screen-and-nameplate closeups showing the five functions on identical hardware. Clear generous layout, the hardware is the main subject. One design only, no alternative silhouettes.
Physical geometry: 1.50 m total width, 1.00 m total depth, 1.40 m total height. The front working edge is 1.02 m above floor. A 1.12 m wide by 0.42 m tall actual inset glass display is inclined 60 degrees above horizontal, its upper edge near 1.40 m. A shallow lower tactile control deck sits in front of it, with ONE rotary controller and FOUR small rectangular physical keys; same layout in all views. Two solid structural side cheeks carry the display and deck down to a broad low recessed plinth. The console has a compact enclosed central body and a small recessed toe area. The back is enclosed by a removable maintenance panel; concealed power/data feed enters from the floor, a single sealed access cover. No chair or knee-sized desk hole. Layered metal construction, modest practical grab edges, few visible fasteners. A small warm white task light lights the control deck.
Materials and palette: graphite/dark blue-grey matte powder-coated body, light grey slightly warm metal support frame, restrained brushed-metal edging, black anti-reflective screen, subtle cyan status line and crisp white text. Gentle wear only at contact edges, no rust or gratuitous grime. Colors harmonize with a dark blue-grey space station and its cyan/orange HUD. Amber only for an actual caution indicator; no rainbow color coding by function. No oversized bloom.
Hero screen: power-supply overview on a real integrated screen, with simple clean diagram of a reactor, two solar arrays and two battery-bank cards. Show only a FEW large readings; use plausible illustrative content, not dense tiny fake prose. Main label on screen and FRONT-facing physical plaque reads exactly "STROMVERSORGUNG". Include readable illustrative values "01 AKTIV 50%" and "02 AUS" on screen. A discreet small device tag says "P-05". Front plaque is physically inset, straight, opaque-backed, right way up to a person standing in front; no floating signs.
Bottom strip exact function names: "DROHNEN", "STROMVERSORGUNG", "STATIONSPLANUNG", "FORSCHUNG", "FERTIGUNG". Each closeup has exactly the same screen/frame proportions and differs only by a simple purposeful UI thumbnail and label. No extra hardware variation.
Board text only: title "STEUERPULT — EINHEITLICHE BAUFORM"; dimension labels "1,50 m", "1,00 m", "1,40 m". Small footer "KONZEPT V1". Use a clean technical sans-serif. Labels sparse, precise and readable.
Lighting: soft neutral studio-quality fill and subtle station ambient lighting, all surfaces visible, no extreme darkness. Calm cyan practical emission, controlled highlights, no cinematic fog.
Constraints: preserve one common coherent design across all angles. Actual physical screens only, no holograms, no levitating elements, no disconnected parts, no tangles of external cables, no exposed fragile pipes, no gigantic buttons, no ornate spaceship greeble clutter, no decorative weapons, no people, no logos or watermark. Entire device visible, no cropping its base. Rendering is a design proposal, not a claim the game has been changed.
